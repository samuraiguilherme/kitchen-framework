# Rubric v1.0.0

Findings require a stable rule ID, supporting passage/file/evidence, consequence, and proposed resolution. Unsupported findings are rejected. Record product assumptions separately from operator answers. Required execution failures and missing evidence are blockers; a reviewer opinion cannot waive them.

## Acceptance rules

Problem, affected users, desired outcome, constraints, non-goals and observable success evidence agree with the human request. Proposed decisions and open questions are explicit.

## Rejection examples

An agent invents a paid-plan limit or treats its own proposed decision as a user answer.

## Required evidence

Original request, guided human answers, relevant product evidence, intent.md.

## Blocking conditions

Missing outcome or unresolved material product decision.
