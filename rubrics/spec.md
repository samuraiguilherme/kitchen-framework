# Rubric v1.0.0

Findings require a stable rule ID, supporting passage/file/evidence, consequence, and proposed resolution. Unsupported findings are rejected. Record product assumptions separately from operator answers. Required execution failures and missing evidence are blockers; a reviewer opinion cannot waive them.

## Acceptance rules

Evaluate intent fidelity and implementation readiness separately. Stable AC-n IDs define observable behavior with examples. Permissions, tenant scope, errors, retries, duplicates, boundaries and relevant edge cases are explicit; N/A is justified.

## Rejection examples

A criterion says 'works correctly'; denied requests and duplicate retries have unspecified outcomes.

## Required evidence

Accepted intent, self-contained spec, AC definitions and examples.

## Blocking conditions

Contradictory requirements, undefined authorization or unresolved product behavior.
